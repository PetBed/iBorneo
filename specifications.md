1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC GOALS
1.1 Objective
To establish a modern, trustworthy, and functional web presence for iBorneo Corporate Services PLT. The site serves as the primary digital gateway for local and international business owners seeking SSM incorporation, corporate secretarial compliance, and business licensing in Sabah.

1.2 Target Audience
Local Entrepreneurs & Startups: Seeking fast, hassle-free incorporation (Sdn Bhd, LLP, Enterprise).

Established SMEs: Looking to switch company secretaries, streamline compliance, or access digital portal solutions.

Foreign Investors & West Malaysian Companies: Expanding operations into Sabah needing registered addresses, local trading licenses, and local regulatory expertise.

1.3 Key Value Propositions
Sabah Expertise: In-depth knowledge of local municipal licensing (DBKK / local councils) and Sabah-specific regulatory nuances.

Digital-First Compliance: Secure 24/7 client portal access for corporate secretarial files and statutory records.

Full Lifecycle Support: From company formation to annual MBRS filings and eventual strike-off/closure.

Primary Teal (#38a3a5 / Brand 500): Represents modern technology, growth, and trust. Derived from the leaf element in the logo.

Deep Corporate Blue (#22577a / Brand 600): Represents stability, professionalism, and legal authority. Derived from the mountain/lettermark in the logo.

Dark Navy / Slate (#0f172a / Slate 900): Used for contrast sections, footers, and crisp typography.
