Frequently asked questions

Answers about company incorporation and secretarial compliance in Sabah.

What is required to incorporate a Sdn Bhd company in Sabah?

A Sdn Bhd requires at least one director and one shareholder (who may be the same person), a valid Malaysian residential address, IC or passport copies, and a proposed company name. iBorneo can handle the SSM name search and incorporation filings.

Do I need a local trading licence (DBKK) in Kota Kinabalu?

Businesses operating physical premises or commercial activities in Kota Kinabalu may need a DBKK trading licence. iBorneo can assist with the application process and local requirements.

How does the digital secretarial service work?

Statutory documents, board resolutions and SSM submission records can be stored in a secure cloud portal for clients to view, download or share.

Can West Malaysian or foreign business owners register a company in Sabah?

West Malaysian and foreign owners may be able to register a company in Sabah, subject to the applicable requirements for the business and sector. iBorneo can discuss local registered-address and secretarial needs.
