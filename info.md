5. Frequently Asked Questions
Common answers regarding company incorporation and secretarial compliance in Sabah.

a) What is required to incorporate a Sdn Bhd company in Sabah?
To set up a Sdn Bhd, you need a minimum of 1 Director and 1 Shareholder (who can be the same person), a valid Malaysian residential address, IC or Passport copies, and a proposed company name. iBorneo Corporate Services PLT handles all SSM name reservation and incorporation filings.

b) Do I need a Local Trading License (DBKK) for my business in Kota Kinabalu?
Yes, if you operate physical premises or commercial activities in Kota Kinabalu, you are required by law to apply for a Trading License with Dewan Bandaraya Kota Kinabalu (DBKK). We assist clients with the full application procedure.

c) How does the Digital Secretarial Service work?
Instead of waiting for physical mail or paper copies, all key statutory documents, board resolutions, and SSM submission receipts are uploaded to your secure cloud portal. You can view, download, or share them 24/7 with your banker or auditor.

d) Can West Malaysian or foreign business owners register a company in Sabah?
Yes! Non-residents and West Malaysian entrepreneurs can hold 100% equity in most business sectors. We provide a registered office address in Kota Kinabalu and advise on local statutory compliance.
